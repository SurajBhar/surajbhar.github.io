I design and develop **artificial intelligence systems that translate frontier research into production-ready applications**. My work is guided by the conviction that the future of AI lies in **human-centred, trustworthy, and sustainable technologies**—systems that combine scientific rigour with practical deployment across diverse domains. 

My academic formation in *Mechatronics (M.Sc.) at Universität Siegen*, with a specialization in Artificial Intelligence, Unsupervised and Self-Supervised Deep Learning, and Software Engineering for intelligent systems, culminated in a Master’s dissertation entitled *Improved Driver Distraction Detection Using Self-Supervised Learning*, supervised by [Prof. Michael Möller](https://sites.google.com/site/michaelmoellermath/), [Dr. Jovita Lukasik](https://jovitalukasik.github.io/) and [M.Sc. David Lerch](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=BDOfyn8AAAAJ). The thesis introduced the **Clustered Feature Weighting (CFW) algorithm**, which improved cross-modality adaptation in driver distraction detection by over seven percent. Conducted in collaboration with *Fraunhofer IOSB*, this research was published at **IEEE ITSC 2025** and contributed to the advancement of multimodal computer vision and interpretable intelligent transportation systems. 

At [Fraunhofer IOSB](https://www.iosb.fraunhofer.de/), under the mentorship of [Dr.-Ing. Michael Voit](https://www.iosb.fraunhofer.de/en/press/press-releases/2024/human-ai-interaction-new-department-focus.html), [Dr.-Ing. Frederik Diederichs](https://www.iosb.fraunhofer.de/de/projekte-produkte/integrated-autonomous-driving-lab.html), and [M.Sc. David Lerch](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=BDOfyn8AAAAJ), I contributed to the [KARLI](https://karli-projekt.de/) and [SALSA](https://projekt-salsa.de/en/) projects in Human–AI Interaction. My work encompassed the application of **vision–language models (CLIP-ViP, Omnivore)** to sleep stage recognition and driver attention analysis, the design of **multimodal ELT pipelines** for large-scale heterogeneous data, and the development of a **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) chatbot** with RAPTOR integration, deployed via GitLab CI/CD. Taken together, these endeavours advanced the integration of **computer vision, large language models, retrieval-based architectures, and interpretable machine learning**, demonstrating the breadth of my contributions across multimodal AI, human–machine interaction, and autonomous systems.  

Within the [Visual Computing Group](https://www.vc.informatik.uni-siegen.de/) led by [Prof. Margret Keuper](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/research/focus-groups/computer-vision-machine-learning-prof-dr-ing-margret-keuper/), I investigated **out-of-distribution robustness, Neural Radiance Fields (NeRFs), and implicit 3D representations**. I implemented **Neural Unsigned Distance Fields (NDFs)** augmented with vector quantisation and integrated latent diffusion models for 3D representation learning, contributing to ongoing research in generalisable implicit representations. In parallel, I attended the *Unsupervised Deep Learning* course and the *Computer Vision Seminar* under Prof. Keuper, where I explored **DIVeR: Neural Radiance Fields with Deterministic Integration for Volume Rendering** and authored a seminar paper on the subject, deepening my understanding of cutting-edge approaches to generalisation and representation in computer vision.  

At present, I extend these foundations as an **independent AI Engineer**, where I design **cloud-native ML pipelines** and **end-to-end MLOps/LLMOps frameworks** deployed across AWS, Azure and GCP. My portfolio projects, documented [here](https://surajbhar.github.io/projects/), exemplify my ability to take models from research through to production, integrating **CI/CD automation, monitoring, and distributed inference**. In addition, I explore **multi-agent systems, agentic AI frameworks, retrieval-augmented generation, and chatbot architectures**, positioning my work at the confluence of modern AI research and real-world system integration.
